Daily

A calm home screen for a jailbroken Kindle: your day (time, weather, to-do, calendar) and your reading (long reads, a quote of the day, news on your topics).

Install

  1. Jailbreak your Kindle with the guide at kindlemodding.org (install the hotfix it offers).
  2. Download daily-kindle.zip and unzip it: you get daily, documents and extensions.
  3. Connect the Kindle by USB and copy the three folders to the top level of the Kindle drive. Choose merge, not replace.
  4. Eject. On the Kindle, type Daily in the search bar and tap it (it's in your library too).

Daily updates itself after that. Optional settings (Google Calendar, Telegram to-dos) go in daily/config.lua: see the README.

Install with KPM (terminal)

  1. In kterm: /var/local/kmc/bin/kpm add-repo https://vimalnaugain.github.io/kindle-daily/app/manifest.json
  2. Then: /var/local/kmc/bin/kpm update and /var/local/kmc/bin/kpm -y install daily
